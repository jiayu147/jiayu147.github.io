Research

Coastal Wetland Ecology & Conservation Biology

Research Overview

My research focuses on understanding the ecological processes that shape coastal wetland ecosystems, with particular emphasis on plant invasions, phenotypic plasticity, and ecosystem responses to environmental change. I combine field observations, controlled experiments, and quantitative analyses to address fundamental and applied questions in coastal ecology.

Coastal wetlands are among the most productive and economically important ecosystems on Earth. However, they face multiple threats from climate change, sea-level rise, and biological invasions. My work aims to inform conservation and management efforts by providing insights into the mechanisms that govern ecosystem structure and function.

Life-history Plasticity in Response to Sea Level Rise

This project investigates how intertidal salt marsh plants respond to changing salinity and inundation regimes associated with sea-level rise. We examine the role of phenotypic plasticity in mediating plant responses and the implications for ecosystem resilience.

Key Questions:

  • How do salinity and inundation interact to influence plant life-history traits?
  • What is the role of phenotypic plasticity in facilitating adaptation to environmental change?
  • How do these responses vary across the species' range?

Invasive Species Management in Coastal Ecosystems

Duration: [Start Year] - [End Year]

This research examines the ecological impacts of invasive plant species in coastal wetlands, with a focus on Spartina alterniflora. We investigate the mechanisms underlying invasion success and develop strategies for effective management and restoration.

Phenotypic Plasticity and Climate Change

Funding: [Funding Agency/Project Number]

Duration: [Start Year] - [End Year]

This project explores the relative importance of climatic drivers versus phenotypic integration in shaping plant responses to environmental gradients. We use common garden experiments and field surveys to disentangle these effects.

Key Questions:

  • How do plants integrate multiple environmental cues?
  • What is the genetic basis of phenotypic plasticity?
  • How will climate change affect plant fitness and distribution?
