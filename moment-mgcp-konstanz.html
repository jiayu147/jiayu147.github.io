2026-04-02Academic Exchange

MGCP Conference in Konstanz University

Jiayu attended the 1st Multiple Global Changes on Plants Conference during 30 March-2 April in Konstanz, Germany, presenting a talk about “Multiple global change factors modulate the size-dependent flowering switch”.

An exciting, unforgettable, colorful trip! It was the first time to go abroad of my own. The conference was held by an excellent phD team (Chinese students) from Ecology group of Mark van Kleunen, University of Konstanz.
